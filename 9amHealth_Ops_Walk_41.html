Workforce Management (WFM)
Two confirmed gaps — structured adherence reporting and real-time agent state visibility — are directly addressed by WFM.
CapabilityWhat It Means for This Program
Adherence ReportingAutomated end-of-day reports showing whether each agent was on the phones, in wrap-up, or in a non-productive state during scheduled hours.
Real-Time Agent MonitoringLive dashboard showing every agent’s current state. Supervisors can act immediately when agents fall into non-productive states.
Volume ForecastingUses historical call volume to predict future demand by hour and day. Enables proactive staffing decisions.
Leadership ReportingStructured weekly and monthly reports on adherence, occupancy, and staffing efficiency for VP-level visibility.
